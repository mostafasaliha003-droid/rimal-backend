## Partner's answers for pages 6 and 7

**Hotel important information:** We have replaced the repeated field-column layout with responsive policy cards. Each supplier entry keeps its own price, original currency, charging basis and calculation together. Children's age ranges and service conditions have readable labels. All supplied nested fields and metapolicy_extra_info remain visible, including zero, false and unspecified values. The layout supports Arabic RTL and English LTR. The display does not infer that an unspecified charge is free or that every fee is payable at the property.

**Room static data:** We match all fields of the complete dynamic rg_ext against the hotel's static room_groups. We do not match by room_group_id or room name. Photographs come only from the exactly matched static room group, preferring images_ext and retaining legacy images. Generic hotel images and arbitrary dynamic-rate images cannot supply room photographs. Unmatched dynamic classifications remain separate and have no borrowed images. The static content lookup is also scoped to the RateHawk provider. In the checked Sandbox example, 15 of 18 rates had an exact static match; the other three correctly remained without static photographs. We inspected sample matched supplier photographs and confirmed that those samples depict rooms.
