# Mkhadda design system and booking-path redesign · 0.26

Source of truth for values: `src/theme.ts` (tokens), `src/components/format.ts` (numbers, dates, counted nouns), `src/components/iconData.ts` (icons, generated by `scripts/generate-icon-data.cjs`). Components live in `src/components/Primitives.tsx`.

## What the redesign optimizes for

A traveler must be able to answer three questions on the review screen before continuing, without expanding anything: **How much will I pay in total? What does my booking include? Until when can I cancel, and at what cost?** The review screen opens with a "Your booking at a glance" card that answers exactly these, followed by the itemized summary and price breakdown.

Return visits are the second goal: the app remembers where the traveler stopped ("Continue your search", resumable in one tap with fresh prices) and surfaces the next trip with its voucher on Explore.

## Decisions taken from the references

| Reference | Decision for Mkhadda |
| --- | --- |
| Booking.com / Agoda / Trip.com | Dense horizontal result cards with fixed slots (stars → name → area → full-stay total with the stay it covers). Edit dates and guests in place from results and hotel pages instead of returning to search. Free cancellation as a one-tap filter next to meal plans. |
| Airbnb | Save with a filled heart, an animated confirmation and an "undo"/"view saved" toast. Photo view toggle; recently viewed marker on return. |
| Apple HIG | 44 pt minimum targets on iOS, sheets for pickers, respect Reduce Motion, mirror only direction-bearing icons. |
| Material 3 | 48 dp targets on Android, window size classes (compact < 600 ≤ medium < 840 ≤ expanded), emphasized/standard easing, top navigation instead of a bottom bar on desktop. |
| WCAG 2.2 AA | Contrast table below, visible focus (2 px ring via `:focus-visible`), Label in Name enforced in `Button`/`Chip`, no layout shift while photos load, status changes announced. |
| Baymard checkout research | One summary for stay, guests, inclusions, cancellation and money; fees payable at the hotel shown next to the total (never hidden); price changes shown as old vs new with explicit acceptance; uncertain payment states shown as a tracker with "do not pay again" guidance. |

## Typography

IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic (its Latin glyphs keep mixed Arabic/English hotel names harmonious); Manrope for English UI. Arabic steps carry taller line heights for diacritics, and `Txt` raises any legacy line height below 1.45× in Arabic.

| Step | Size | Line height EN / AR | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| display | 30 | 38 / 44 | Bold |
| title | 24 | 32 / 36 | Bold |
| heading | 20 | 28 / 32 | Bold |
| subheading | 17 | 24 / 28 | Bold |
| body / bodyStrong | 16 | 24 / 28 | Regular / Medium |
| label / small | 14 | 20 / 24 | Bold / Regular |
| caption | 13 | 18 / 20 | Regular |
| micro | 12 | 16 / 20 | Medium |
| price / priceLarge | 22 / 28 | 28 / 32 · 36 / 40 | Bold |

Numbers, dates and money (`format.ts`): Western digits in both languages, so a date, a night count and a total never mix digit systems on one line; day-first dates (`14 Dec`, `14 ديسمبر`); prices as `USD 1,585.92` isolated left-to-right inside Arabic, grouped and padded to the currency's minor unit but **never rounded** (supplier precision such as `EUR 0.0123` is kept). Arabic counted nouns use singular/dual/plural forms and the genitive after prepositions (`في غرفتين`). Long hotel names wrap to two lines on cards and in full on the hotel page (verified at 320 px).

## Spacing, shape, elevation, layout

- Spacing (4-point): 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64.
- Radius: 6, 8, 12 (controls), 16 (cards), 24 (sheets, hero), pill.
- Borders: hairline, 1, 1.5, 2 (focus).
- Elevation: `card` (1 px, 6%), `raised` (sticky bars), `overlay` (sheets, toast).
- Touch targets: 44 pt iOS / web, 48 dp Android (`touch.min`).
- Layout: gutters 16 (12 below 360 px) / 24 / 32; reading width 720, forms 560, sheets 640, content 1200. Desktop gets top navigation, two-column home and review, and a two-column results grid; phones keep the bottom tab bar.
- Motion: 120 / 200 / 320 ms with Material standard/emphasized easing. Every animation checks `useReducedMotion()`; the web build also clamps CSS transitions under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Push transitions follow reading direction (`slide_from_left` in Arabic).

## Iconography

63 Lucide 1.47.0 icons (ISC, same set as the website) rendered with `react-native-svg`: 24×24 grid, round caps and joins, stroke 1.75 (2 at ≤ 18 px for equal optical weight), colored through `currentColor`. Icons are decorative; the owning control carries the accessible name. Only `chevron`, `back`, `forward` and `logout` mirror in right-to-left layouts.

## Components and states

`Button` (primary, secondary, quiet, tonal, danger; pressed, hover, focus, disabled, busy), `IconButton` (required label), `Chip` (choice, checkbox, radio, removable filter), `Badge`, `Notice` (what happened + what to do), `Card`, `Field` (focus ring, error with icon), `Photo` (reserved footprint, fade-in, fallback), `Skeleton`, `StatePanel`, `MoneyText`, `DisclosureMark`, `Toast`, `StaySummaryBar`, `DateFields`, `GuestsField`, `useStayEditor`.

Covered states: offline, timeout, rate limit, server error, session expired (search resumes after sign-in), membership required/under review/needs information/approved/declined/suspended, no results (change dates in place), filter dead-end (one-tap removal of the filter that blocks results, dates and guests kept), sold-out hotel, missing photos, price change (old vs new, explicit acceptance), payment request in progress/unknown/failed/verified, booking pending vs confirmed, past booking (book again starts a fresh, rechecked search).

## Verification (fixture data, Expo web export)

All API calls were answered by a local fixture router built from the real request bodies; booking-creation and payment endpoints were blocked. These are browser renderings of the Expo app, not device screenshots.

- Family scenario (2 adults + 2 children in 2 rooms, ages 8 and 5, 3 nights) in Arabic and English at 390 px and 1440 px, 10/10 assertions each: request occupancy and dates, stay bar on results/hotel/review, card total = hotel "from" total, scroll position restored after returning from a hotel, dates changed in place with guests kept and totals repriced, free-cancellation filter with a local-time deadline, review answers and per-room guests, price recheck.
- 17 state scenarios (returning visit, trips, membership states, no results, filter dead-end, offline, price change, sold out, broken photos, 320 px AR/EN, 768 px).
- Keyboard-only path (sign in → search → hotel → review) with visible focus rings.
- axe-core 4.14 WCAG 2.2 A/AA rules: 0 violations on 14 scans (7 screens × AR phone / EN desktop).
- 295 unit tests, TypeScript, Expo lint, expo-doctor 20/20, Android Hermes bundle export.

## Needs a separate decision or external work

- `react-native-svg` is a native module: an Android/iOS build is required before device testing. No native build or device test was performed.
- Guest review score and count are not in the search card response; cards show supplier stars only. Showing reviews needs a backend `review_summary` (e.g. from the synced reviews dump).
- Trip cards cannot show address, map or check-in instructions because `/user/profile` bookings carry no hotel id; the voucher contains them when the supplier provides them. Meal plan and free-cancellation deadline exist in the profile response but are excluded by the app's privacy whitelist (Data Safety review needed before adding).
- Booking and payment still complete on the Remal website while native checkout stays disabled.
