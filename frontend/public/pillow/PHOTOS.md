# Website photography

The city photographs illustrate the public front of the website. Hotel and room galleries use supplier photographs.

- `hero-marina.jpg`, `card-marina.jpg`, `share.jpg` (background): Dubai Marina at twilight, photographed by Yourusernamewillbepublic2 and released under CC0. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dubai_Marina_and_Sheikh_Zayed_Road_from_Saba_3_JLT.jpg)
- `hero-istanbul.jpg`, `card-world.jpg`: Istanbul, Ortaköy Mosque and the Bosphorus Bridge. Gokhan, released into the public domain. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ortakoy_Camii_ve_Kopru.jpg)
- `hero-paris.jpg`: Paris, the Eiffel Tower. BigBartimäus, released into the public domain. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eiffelturm.JPG)
- `hero-jumeirah.jpg`, `card-jumeirah.jpg`: crops of `dubai.jpg` (Burj Al Arab, Jumeirah), the photograph already used on the website.

All files are crops and re-encodes of those originals; nothing else was changed.
