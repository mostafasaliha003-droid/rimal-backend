# Website photography

The city photographs illustrate the public front of the website. Hotel and room galleries use supplier photographs.

- `hero-marina.webp`, `card-marina.webp`: Dubai Marina at sunset, photographed by Maaroo24 and released under CC0. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dubai_Marina_27.12.2024.jpg)
- `hero-istanbul.webp`, `card-world.webp`: Istanbul's historic peninsula (Sultan Ahmed Mosque, Hagia Sophia) and the Bosphorus, photographed by Hunanuk and released under CC0. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Historical_peninsula_and_modern_skyline_of_Istanbul.jpg)
- `hero-paris.webp`: Paris, the Eiffel Tower. BigBartimäus, released into the public domain. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eiffelturm.JPG)
- `share.jpg` (background): Dubai Marina at twilight, photographed by Yourusernamewillbepublic2 and released under CC0. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dubai_Marina_and_Sheikh_Zayed_Road_from_Saba_3_JLT.jpg)
- `hero-jumeirah.webp`, `card-jumeirah.webp`: crops of `dubai.jpg` (Burj Al Arab, Jumeirah), the photograph already used on the website. Its source and licence are not recorded here and should be confirmed by the owner.

All files are crops and re-encodes (WebP) of those originals; nothing else was changed.
